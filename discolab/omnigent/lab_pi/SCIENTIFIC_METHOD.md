# Scientific-method doctrine

This bundle follows the detailed playbook in
`docs/SCIENTIFIC_RESEARCH_PLAYBOOK.md`. Operationally:

1. State the estimand, unit, comparator, mechanism, smallest important effect,
   and observation that would falsify the hypothesis.
2. Separate exploration/development from held-out confirmation. Freeze every
   threshold, model, stopping rule, and analysis choice before confirmation.
3. Prediction is not causation. A predictive signal earns an intervention only
   through a mechanism-matched control (including equal average intensity).
4. Preserve pairing and independent units; audit leakage, dependence,
   multiplicity, censoring, missingness, measurement error, and external validity.
5. Report effects with uncertainty, practical significance, tail risk, negative
   results, deviations, and unresolved comparisons.
6. Adaptive experimentation must be compared with a fixed/exhaustive strategy
   on compute-to-conclusion *and* conclusion agreement.
7. Every number comes from tools; every claim has provenance; every decision is
   replayable from the ledger and immutable artifacts.
